Everything hifz asks of you, in one quiet place

Quranio is a tool for your own study. It does not replace a teacher; it helps you arrive prepared.

A plan for today

Choose your minutes. Each day brings one block of new material and the passages that are due, scheduled āyah by āyah with spaced repetition (FSRS).

The full Mushaf

The complete Ḥafṣ text on the printed 15-line page or as flowing text, with tajweed colours, translations and tafsīr.

Listen and repeat

Choose from many reciters, loop an āyah or a range, and follow each word as it is recited.

Test yourself, honestly

Exam mode starts from the first words of an āyah you know. A quiz trains the look-alike āyāt (mutashābihāt). A juz map shows what is firm and what needs care.

Also: works offline once set up · your data stays on your device · backup and restore to a file · the core features are free.

How it works

A few questions at the start, then one clear next step every day.

  1. Tell it where you are

    Start from the beginning, from Juz ʿAmma, or mark the sūrahs you already know.

  2. Give it your minutes

    Set the time you have each day. The plan fits new learning and review inside it.

  3. Recite, then rate

    Reveal the text, mark where you stumbled. Each rating shapes what returns, and when.
